What is a test user?

A test user lets the club owner sign in as you to check that everything on the site works properly — no separate throwaway accounts needed.

🧪 Why it exists

When something breaks or a new feature ships, the owner needs a real account to test it. Test users make that possible without cluttering the club with fake profiles.

👀 What it means for you

  • Your password is visible to the owner on the Users page, so they can sign in as you when testing.
  • Everything else about your account stays exactly the same.
  • It only helps make the site better — your points, lessons, and posts are untouched.

✅ It's optional (mostly)

The box is checked by default, but you can uncheck it when you join. If you opt out, your password stays hidden.

⬆️ Members are auto-enrolled

When you're promoted to Member (or higher), you're automatically added as a test user so the owner can sign in as you to test club features. This keeps the whole club testable end-to-end.

Test access is only ever used to keep Matix running smoothly. Questions? Ask an owner in the club. 💙
