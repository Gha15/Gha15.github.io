Delete your account

For the app Matix the math club - math app (com.matixmathclubappandroid.app) and the website matixthemathclub.com, by the developer matix the math club (Ghadi).

How to ask for your account to be deleted

  1. Send an email to ghadimatix@gmail.com, from the email address on your Matix account if you can.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Matix account and write your Matix username.
  3. We reply to confirm the account is yours, then delete it.
Email us to delete my account

No email on your account? Write to us anyway with your username, or report it from the Bugs page in the app, and we will help you.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username, password and email address.
  • Your profile: display name, bio, avatar and profile picture, and your role.
  • Your friends, follows, and comments on your profile.
  • Your game scores, points, lesson progress and saved exercise sets.
  • Posts you made in the club (ideas, discussions, comments, bug reports), when you ask for them to be removed too.

What is kept, and for how long

  • A backup copy of the deleted account is kept for up to 30 days, only so a mistaken deletion can be undone, and is then erased.
  • Math Chat messages are end-to-end encrypted: we cannot read them. Messages you sent stay in the other person’s chat as encrypted text until they delete them.
