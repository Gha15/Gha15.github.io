How_to_Get_Points
Daily_Activities
Complete Lessons
Finish any lesson from the MATIX lessons section and earn points for your dedication to learning.
→ Earn: +10 points
Master Exercises
Successfully complete exercises with high accuracy. The better your performance, the more points you get!
→ Earn: +5 to +15 points
Daily Login Streak
Visit MATIX every day and maintain your learning streak. Consecutive days multiply your rewards!
→ Earn: +1 point per day (streak bonuses available)
Gaming_Achievements
Math Fight Victories
Win matches in Math Fight and prove your calculation speed against others.
→ Earn: +10 points per win
Math Plane Game Levels
Complete levels in The Math Plane Game. Higher difficulties earn more points!
→ Earn: Easy +5 | Medium +10 | Hard +20 points
Special_Contributions
Help Community
Answer questions in the FAQ section or help other members with their math problems.
→ Earn: +5 points per helpful contribution
Special Events
Participate in special MATIX events, challenges, and competitions announced periodically.
→ Earn: Variable (up to +100 points)
