Math Fight

Keep running. Challenge others. Last heart wins.

Rules

  • Move with WASD / Arrow Keys / joystick on mobile
  • When it's your turn → click a player to challenge them
  • They get a multiplication question and 12 seconds to answer
  • They answer correctly → you lose a life ❤️
  • They answer wrong or time out → they lose a life ❤️
  • Everyone starts with 3 lives ❤️❤️❤️
  • Last player standing wins!
