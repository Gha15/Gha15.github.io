Rules

  • Players split into two teams: Red vs Blue.
  • Every round everyone gets a random math question.
  • In each team, the first to answer wrong is out.
  • If nobody's wrong, the slowest correct answer is out.
  • Repeat until one Red and one Blue remain.
  • Finalists box in the Purple ring with math hits (+ - × ÷).
  • Each hit knocks the rival back - harder every time, a bit random.
  • Shove them out of purple into their color to win (sumo style)!
  • Not enough players? MathBots fill the room - and get replaced when real players join.
