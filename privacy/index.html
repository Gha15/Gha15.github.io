This privacy policy explains how matix the math club (“we”, “us”), the developer of the Android app Matix the math club - math app (package com.matixmathclubappandroid.app) and the website matixthemathclub.com (together, “Matix”), collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use Matix. The app and the website use the same Matix account, so this policy covers both.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: a username and a password. The password is stored in an obfuscated (scrambled) form and is used only to sign you in.
  • Email address: used to verify your account, to sign in with your email, and to send you one-time codes (verification, password reset, email change). Only a hash of each code is stored, and codes expire after 15 minutes.
  • Profile: display name, a short bio, an avatar emoji or a profile picture you choose to upload, and a favourite colour. These are visible to other members.
  • Posts: discussions, ideas, comments, bug reports and lesson requests you write. These are not encrypted and are visible to club members.
  • Matix AI questions: the questions you type into Matix AI, the Translator and “Teach me anything”.
  • Accounts made by a teacher: a teacher can make student accounts for their class. The teacher types the student’s first name; we store it with a username, a password, the class name and the school name. No email is needed for these accounts.

Information created when you use Matix

  • Progress and scores: video lessons watched, quiz stars, game scores and best scores, points, badges and leaderboard positions.
  • Homework: for students in a class, the homework their teacher gives and their homework scores.
  • Social activity: friends, friend requests, follows, notifications, online status (“online now”) and typing indicators in Math Chat.
  • Call set-up: when you start or accept a voice or video call, the short-lived connection details needed to connect the two devices are passed through our database.
  • On-device settings: language, theme, text size and similar preferences are stored only on your device.

Device permissions

  • Camera and microphone: requested only when you start or answer a call in Math Chat, or take a profile picture. You can refuse; the rest of Matix still works.
  • Internet and network state: to load your account, lessons and chats.

What we do not collect: end-to-end encrypted data

The following is end-to-end encrypted or never leaves your devices. It cannot be read by us, by the club owner, or by our service providers, so it is not collected by Matix:

  • Math Chat messages (text, symbols, stickers and equations). They are encrypted on your device (ECDH P-256 + AES-256-GCM) before they are sent. Our database only passes along encrypted text that we have no key for.
  • Your chat key. It is created and kept on your device. If you choose to back it up, the backup is locked with a passphrase that only you know, and we cannot open it.
  • Voice and video calls. Audio and video go directly between the two devices and are never sent to our servers, recorded or stored.

Matix does not collect your precise location, contacts, phone number, call logs, SMS, advertising ID or payment information. Matix has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in on the app and the website.
  • To show your profile, messages, scores and points to other members of the club, as described below.
  • To save your progress, and to sync your game scores between the app and the website.
  • To answer Matix AI questions and translations.
  • To send account emails (codes only; we do not send marketing emails from the app).
  • To keep the club safe: to fix bugs, and to let the owner ban or remove accounts that break the rules.

3. What other people can see

  • Public to members: your username, display name, bio, avatar or profile picture, role, points, game scores, leaderboard positions, ideas, discussions and comments.
  • Private: your password and your email address.
  • Only you and the person you chat with: your Math Chat messages and calls (end-to-end encrypted; nobody else, including us, can read or hear them).
  • Your teacher: if your account was made by a teacher, that teacher sees your first name, username, class and homework scores, and can give you a new password or delete the account.
  • The club owners can see account details needed to run the club, including the list of schools, teachers, classes and students (for example to help you reset access or to remove an account), but cannot read your chats.

Please do not use your real full name or other personal details as your username.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and club data are kept while your account exists.
  • One-time email codes expire after 15 minutes and are deleted when used.
  • When an account is deleted, a backup is kept for up to 30 days so a mistaken deletion can be undone, and is then deleted.

6. Deleting your account and data

Step-by-step instructions and the full list of what is deleted: matixthemathclub.com/delete-account.

You can ask us to delete your Matix account and the data linked to it (account, email, profile, posts, scores and friends) at any time:

  • email ghadimatix@gmail.com from the email on your account, with the subject “Delete my Matix account” and your username, or
  • report it from the Bugs page in the app.

7. Security

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Math Chat messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted, so they cannot be read on our servers. Passwords are stored obfuscated, never in plain text, and email codes are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

8. Children

Matix is a study app for school students. We ask for only the information needed to run the club and do not show ads or track users. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, please use Matix with a parent’s or teacher’s permission. If a parent believes their child has given us personal information without permission, contact us and we will delete it. Student accounts made by a teacher hold only a first name, and the teacher or a parent can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell members in the app for important changes.
