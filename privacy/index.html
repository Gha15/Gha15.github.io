Privacy

We keep things simple: we only use the little bit of data we need to make the site work. Here is what that means.

What is private

  • Your password. It is only used to check that it is really you when you log in or change something, and it is never shown to other people.
  • Your email address and anything you type into the contact form. Only the club owner reads it, to reply to you.
  • Anything saved only on your own device (for example, which accounts you joined on this browser). It stays in your browser.

What is partly private

Some data is visible to other people, but only a small part of it.

  • Usernames. Your username can be seen by other members, for example on the users page, in games and in chat. Other people can see the name, but not your password or your email.
  • Because a username is public, please do not use your real full name or other personal details in it. Pick a nickname instead.
  • Game scores and points shown next to a username are visible too.

Why this data is used

  • Passwords: to keep your account safe so nobody else can pretend to be you.
  • Email and contact messages: to answer your questions and fix bugs you report.
  • Usernames: so members can recognize each other, play together, follow each other and appear on leaderboards. The tradeoff is that a username has to be visible for this to work, so choose one you are comfortable sharing.
  • Scores and progress: to remember how you are doing in the games and puzzles.
