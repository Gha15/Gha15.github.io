📦 How to install it

🪟 Windows

  1. Download matix-win.zip
  2. Right-click it → Extract All. You can’t run the installer from inside the zip
  3. Open matix-installer.exe
  4. If a blue warning appears, see getting past the warnings
  5. Matix shows up in your Start menu 🎉

🍎 Mac

  1. Download matix-mac.zip and double-click to unzip it
  2. Open the .dmg inside
  3. Drag Matix into your Applications folder
  4. The first launch needs a small trick — see getting past the warnings
  5. After that, open it like any other app

🤖 Android

  1. Download matix-android.zip and unzip it to get matix.apk
  2. Tap the APK
  3. Your phone will ask permission to install from outside the Play Store — allow it
  4. Done!

No unzip app on your phone? Files by Google opens zips, or download it on a computer and send it over.

🛡️ Getting past the security warnings

Windows, Mac and Android all warn about Matix, and that’s expected. It doesn’t mean anything is wrong with it.

It happens because Matix isn’t “code signed” — a certificate that costs hundreds of dollars a year. Matix is a school math club app, not a company. Every small app hits this.

🌐 First — your browser may block the download

Chrome and Edge sometimes say “not commonly downloaded”, or look like the download just failed.

  1. Open your Downloads list (Ctrl+J)
  2. Find Matix and click the ⋯ or arrow beside it
  3. Choose Keep or Keep anyway

🪟 Windows

When the blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears:

  1. Click More info — small text, easy to miss
  2. Click Run anyway

No “Run anyway” button? Windows blocked the file itself:

  1. Right-click matix-installer.exe → Properties
  2. At the bottom, tick Unblock
  3. Click OK and open it again

🍎 Mac

Mac says “Matix can’t be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software.”

Try this first:

  1. Right-click (or Control-click) Matix → Open
  2. Click Open in the box that appears

If that doesn’t work — newer macOS versions removed that shortcut:

  1. Try opening Matix normally, then close the warning
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security
  3. Scroll to Security — you’ll see “Matix was blocked from use”
  4. Click Open Anyway
  5. Enter your password or Touch ID, then click Open

You only do this once.

🤖 Android

  1. When you tap the APK, your phone says this source isn’t allowed → tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap the APK again
  2. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app → tap Install anyway (it may be hidden behind More details)

🔄 How to update

New versions get posted on the releases page.

  • 🪟 Windows — just run the new installer. It updates your existing Matix in place, no uninstalling needed.
  • 🍎 Mac — open the new .dmg and drag Matix into Applications again. Choose Replace when asked.
  • 🤖 Android — you do need to uninstall Matix first, then install the new APK. Android refuses to install over the old one.
💾

You won’t lose anything. Your games, ideas, bug reports, lessons, points and profile all live on your Matix account, not on your device. Sign back in and everything is exactly where you left it.

❓ Questions

Is Matix safe? Why does my computer warn me?

Yes. The warning appears on every app that isn’t code signed, and signing costs hundreds of dollars a year — which a school math club doesn’t have. The warning is about a missing certificate, not about anything detected in the app. Follow the steps above to get past it.

Do I need an account? How do I get one?

Yes — Matix asks you to sign in when it opens. Accounts are given out to club members, so ask Ghadi for yours.

Why does it ask me to sign in every time I open it?

Matix doesn’t keep you logged in between launches. Nothing is wrong and nothing was lost — sign in again and everything is still there.

Do I need internet to use it?

Yes. Your games, lessons, ideas and profile are stored online so they follow you between devices, so Matix needs a connection to sign in and load your stuff.

Is there an iPhone or iPad version?

Not yet. Apple only allows installing apps through the App Store unless you pay for a developer account, so there’s no way to hand out an iPhone version for free right now. On an iPhone, open Matix in your browser instead.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Install it anywhere you like and sign in with the same account — your work follows you.

Windows says something about WebView2

Matix uses a Microsoft component called WebView2 to draw the app. It’s already on almost every up-to-date Windows 10 and 11 PC, and the installer sets it up if it’s missing. On an older PC you can install the free “Evergreen Standalone Installer” from Microsoft’s WebView2 page and then reopen Matix.

Something’s broken. Where do I report it?

Open the Bugs tab inside Matix and post it. You can reply to other people’s reports too — so if the same thing is happening to you, say so. It really helps track down what’s going on.

Can I delete it?

Of course. Uninstall it like any other app — Add or Remove Programs on Windows, drag it to the Trash on Mac, long-press → Uninstall on Android. Your account and everything in it stays safe, so you can come back any time.
